```
Taking advantage by pretending to be another person, or holding a position of authority.
```

# Pretending
You cannot actively claim to be--or present yourself as--another player.[^1][^2][^4]

This includes:
-  Anonymous mail presenting yourself as the target player, faction leadership, etc.[^2]

This *may* include:
- Changing your profile to match the target player[^3]

This does *not* include:
- Changing your name to mimic another player[^1]
	- Even if you know people will mistake you for that other player[^4]

# Taking Advantage
Passively mimicking another player may still violate the rule if you benefit from it. E.g.:
- "very obviously copying everything from a profile **for the purpose of your own enrichment**"[^3]
- "he's not claiming to be the real you or using the name for any kind of leverage/harassment, so it doesn't meet the threshold for action on our part."[^1]

# Cases
## Legal
- [D3vl & D3v1](https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=threads&f=17&t=16504485)
	- Copied username and faction name
- GalloInfligo & GalloInfIigo
	- Copied username. Received items intended for the other player were returned. No penalty issued, but name was changed upon being deemed a [bug](https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=threads&f=4&t=16606374).
## Illegal
- "Someone sending anon messages claiming to be another person, a faction leader for example, giving false instructions in a war"[^2]
- "Someone setting their name as similar to a staff member and then messaging people saying they're going to ban them"[^2]
