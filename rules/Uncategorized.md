Miscellaneous rules that don't appear to be attached to anything on the rules page.

1. Staff may be targeted using intended game mechanics (e.g., bounties, dirty bombs) EXCEPT as retribution for a staff action[^1]
2. [Committee abuse handling](https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=threads&f=1&t=15990764)
3. Staff[^2][^3] and [Player Committee](https://www.torn.com/committee.php#/step=main)[^2][^3] aren't allowed to scam, or otherwise defect on an agreement in a way that undermines trust in their official position
	- This does not apply to Elimination, as defection is expected[^6]
4. Staff, Committee, and other positions with non-public information may not use that information for personal benefit (insider trading)[^4][^5]
