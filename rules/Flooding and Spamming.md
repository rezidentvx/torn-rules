# Off-Topic Discussion
Forum threads must be in-scope for the forum, and posts must be on-topic for the thread.

"Off-topic" is difficult to qualify as there is no clear guidance, rulings in both directions are questionable and inconsistent, and off-topic content is often removed and therefore unavailable for reference.

Some topics that may be considered unrelated to Torn (thus belonging in the [Non-related forums](https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=forums&f=700)) are exempted and allowed in [General Discussion](https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=forums&f=2) at staff discretion.[^2]
