```
Users are permitted to own only one Torn account and should never register a duplicate for any reason whatsoever. Using more than one account can give someone an unfair advantage, so Torn staff aggressively monitor suspected abuse using a huge array of tools developed over the last decade. A first-time multiple account offense will result in a game ban or deletion depending on the severity, with all illegal gains being removed.
```

# Multi-like Behavior
Even if two accounts are operated exclusively by two different people, their activities may still violate the intent of the multi rule and be penalized accordingly.

This does not preclude normal friendly behavior, such as:
- Giving them a head start or helping them[^3]
- Trading or other regular interaction[^3]
- Joining the same faction or company[^3]

"\[A\]s long as you're genuinely separate people only using your respective accounts that you yourselves made then there's no issue. You can interact with them as you would any other player."[^3]

## Feeding
An account may not exist only to funnel resources to another.[^2] Staff have internal criteria to qualify this.[^2]

# Restarts
Any player wishing to restart must follow the instructions [here](https://wiki.torn.com/wiki/NewAccount#Delete_an_account).

As the linked instructions are ~~complete,~~ authoritative, and subject to change, they won't be repeated here. But some key points:
- You must close the original account, wait the allotted time, and then finalize the deletion BEFORE creating any new account.
- You cannot transfer any assets to the new account, even indirectly ([middlemanning](./Multiple%20Accounts#Middlemanning)), or assign a referrer.
- Permafeds must wait 90 days before restarting.
	- It is possible that an individual is not allowed to restart at all. Check with staff first.

## Middlemanning
While this is its own fed reason, it does not have its own rule.

You may NOT assist in transferring any assets from a player that is fedded or restarting to their new account. You MAY receive assets from fedded/restarting players to use as your own (keeping the assets for yourself or transferring them only to other players).

# Perception Is Reality
The multi rule is strongly enforced by the spirit of the rule, as illustrated with multi-like behavior.
- "What I said is that if you flag enough criteria for being deemed multiple accounts, don't be surprised if you're treated like multiple accounts."[^1]
- "The way I always answer this question to people who ask about having a feeder account is if it acts like a duck and quacks like a duck, don’t be surprised if it gets shot like a duck."[^2]
