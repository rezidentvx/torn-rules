Message [me](https://www.torn.com/profiles.php?XID=2077404) in-game or post in [the forum thread](https://www.torn.com/forums.php#/p=threads&f=61&t=16614300).

Looking for:
- **Unanswered questions**. I can do the research. Just tell me what you want to know.
	- This includes anything that's unclear.
- **Material** (evidence, relevant cases, etc.) relating to unanswered questions and/or [TODO](./TODO) items, even if it's not sufficient or reliable enough to justify inclusion.
- **Corrections**
	- ***Incorrect info***. Obviously a massive concern. If I've misinterpreted the material, enforcement has changed, etc., please inform me ASAP.
	- **Ambiguity**. Nothing should be up to interpretation.
	- **Formatting**. Seemingly not a big deal, but can impact clarity and the ability to find information.
- **General requests**. Anything that makes this resource more helpful.

Things to consider:
- For requests, please check [TODO](./TODO) first to ensure it's not already on the list.
- Please include any evidence you can. Speculative and uncited inputs will not be added.
- Falsified contributions are tantamount to [enticement](./rules/Enticement) and will be reported as such.


Thank you to everyone providing info for this project. I can't possibly know every bit of Torn history, every fed, every forum post, etc. that we need to make this happen. 

And with your help, hopefully others won't have to.
